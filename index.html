POWERPLAY RULEBOOK

The rules have changed.

It’s chess — until the first capture.

  • Take a piece and your capturing piece inherits the captured piece’s movement power.
  • Your piece keeps its new power for the rest of the game. Its original power is gone.
  • Kings are the exception. They can’t be captured or transformed; checkmate still wins.
  • Everything else is classic chess: check, castling, en passant, and pawn promotion all apply.

Choose a piece, then tap a highlighted square. Captures are marked with a ring.
